Methodology, Scientific Scope & Limitations

educational standard

Heat Index vs. Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT): HeatGuard calculates the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Heat Index, an apparent temperature metric based on air temperature and relative humidity measured in shaded conditions with light air movement. The Heat Index is not Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT), which directly integrates solar radiation, angle of the sun, and wind speed.

Important Legal & Medical Disclaimer: HeatGuard is an educational prototype. Calculated estimates and visual risk levels do not constitute medical diagnoses, clinical determinations of personal heat vulnerability, or official government disaster declarations.
