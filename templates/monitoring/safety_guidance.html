Emergency Medical Notice & Critical Warning Signs

HeatGuard provides educational information and does not provide personalized medical advice or clinical diagnoses. If you or someone around you exhibits signs of severe heat illness — including: body temperature over 39.5°C (103°F), loss of consciousness, confusion or slurred speech, vomiting, or hot, red, dry skin — CALL LOCAL EMERGENCY SERVICES (e.g. 108 / 112 / 911) IMMEDIATELY.

While awaiting emergency personnel: move the person into shade or air conditioning, loosen tight clothing, and apply cool, damp cloths or ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin. Do NOT force liquids on an unconscious person.
