About HeatGuard

Extreme Heatwave Early Warning & Human Thermal Stress Index

🎯 Project Goal

HeatGuard is a real-time weather intelligence platform that calculates human thermal stress using the Heat Index formula (Steadman / NWS), classifies risk into five danger bands, and provides location-aware early warnings and safety guidance.

🔬 Heat Index Formula

The apparent temperature is calculated using the Rothfusz regression equation (NWS), which combines air temperature (°C) and relative humidity (%) to estimate how hot conditions actually feel to the human body.

Future upgrade: WBGT (Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature) adds radiant heat & wind.

🌡️ Risk Classification

< 27°C🟢 Low – fatigue possible
27–32°C🟡 Caution – heat cramps possible
32–41°C🟠 Extreme Caution – exhaustion likely
41–54°C🔴 Danger – heat stroke probable
> 54°C🔴 Extreme Danger – imminent

🛠️ Tech Stack

  • ⚙️ Backend: Python 3 · Django 6
  • 🎨 Frontend: Tailwind CSS · Chart.js · Leaflet.js
  • 🗄️ Database: SQLite (search history)
  • 🌐 Weather API: Open-Meteo (free, no key required)
  • 📍 Geocoding: Open-Meteo Geocoding API

⚠️ Disclaimer

HeatGuard is a demonstration project for educational purposes. The heat-index risk bands shown are approximate and based on commonly used NWS categories — they are not a complete occupational safety assessment. Always consult local authorities and accredited meteorological services during heatwave events. Do not use this application as the sole basis for health or safety decisions.
